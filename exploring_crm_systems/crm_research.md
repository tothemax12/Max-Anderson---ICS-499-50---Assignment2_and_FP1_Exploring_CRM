# CRM Research

## 1. What is CRM?

### Definition

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is a combination of strategies, processes, and software used by organizations to manage interactions with current and potential customers. CRM systems store customer information, track communications, and help organizations improve customer relationships.

### Purpose

The primary purpose of a CRM system is to centralize customer information and improve interactions between an organization and its customers. CRM systems help businesses increase sales, improve customer satisfaction, and streamline communication.

### History

CRM originated from traditional customer record-keeping methods such as paper files and spreadsheets. During the 1980s and 1990s, businesses began using computerized databases to manage customer information. Early CRM software focused mainly on sales automation.

### Evolution

Modern CRM systems have evolved into cloud-based platforms that integrate sales, marketing, customer service, and analytics. Today's CRM solutions often include automation, artificial intelligence, mobile access, and integration with other business applications.

## 2. Why Do Organizations Use CRM Systems?

### Sales Management

CRM systems help sales teams track leads, manage opportunities, monitor customer interactions, and improve the sales process.

### Customer Management

Organizations use CRM systems to maintain detailed customer records, communication history, preferences, and account information.

### Marketing

CRM platforms assist marketing teams by organizing customer data, segmenting audiences, managing campaigns, and tracking marketing performance.

### Customer Support

Customer service teams use CRM systems to manage support requests, track issues, and provide timely assistance to customers.

### Reporting and Analytics

CRM systems generate reports and dashboards that help managers evaluate sales performance, customer trends, and business metrics.

## 4. Major CRM Modules

### Contacts

Stores information about individual customers, prospects, and business relationships.

### Accounts

Maintains information about companies or organizations associated with contacts.

### Leads

Tracks potential customers who may become future clients.

### Opportunities

Manages potential sales deals and revenue-generating activities.

### Activities

Records customer interactions such as calls, meetings, emails, and appointments.

### Tasks

Tracks action items, reminders, and follow-up responsibilities.

### Marketing Campaigns

Helps plan, execute, and monitor marketing efforts directed at customers and prospects.

### Customer Support Tickets

Tracks customer issues, support requests, and service resolutions.

### Reports

Provides detailed summaries of CRM data and business performance.

### Dashboards

Displays key performance indicators and important business metrics through visual interfaces.
