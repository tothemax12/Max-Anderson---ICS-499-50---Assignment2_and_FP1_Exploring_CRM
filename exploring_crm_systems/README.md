## CRM Research Findings
From this research I learned that a CRM (customer relationship management) system, is, as the name implies, used for managing relationships
with customers. This is accomplished through common CRM features such as contact and account management or a lead tracking page, where we can manage
potential customers.



## CRM Product Comparisons
My in depth CRM product comparison tables can be found in my crm_research.md file,
However, overall, ChatGPT recommended Salesforce for these reasons:

"Extremely powerful and customizable
Excellent lead, contact, opportunity, and support management
Massive integration ecosystem (5,000+ integrations)
Scales from small businesses to large enterprises
Strong reporting, automation, and AI capabilities

The downside is cost and complexity. Many organizations need an administrator or consultant to fully utilize it."
